Maze Runner
THE MOONSTONE RUINS

A ten-stage journey through the Moonstone Ruins and beyond. Find each exit before the 8-minute timer runs out.

01
Brick MazeFind the glowing Solar Gate.
02
Dark HallsReach the Rift; keep clear of the drifting ghost.
03
Moonstone RuinsFind 2 runes; waystones help evade sentinels and lich.
04
Glacier VaultFind the Aurora Arch; warm by braziers and dodge wisps/yeti.
05
Ember ForgeTurn 3 valves; avoid moving pistons and lava vents.
06
Astral Bloom GardenCollect 4 star-moths; moonflowers teleport, gargoyles hunt in Eclipse.
07
Mirror SpireThe Oracle Lens reveals the true gate; beware hidden wraiths.
08
Shifting VaultWalls stay still for 20 seconds, then sections shift in staggered waves about every 20 seconds. Avoid rising slabs and golems; the Vault Door reappears elsewhere.
09
Sunken DepthsRefill air at vents; evade jellyfish, eel and shark.
10
Ice Mountain DescentSki to the finish; dodge hazards; caches, checkpoints and one rescue drone help.

Maze aid: Stages 1–9 hide a one-use hammer that breaks one wall. Fresh maze runs rotate through five layouts.

Controls: Hold either side to steer, or use Left/Right or A/D. Tap the center to stop (the timer and enemies continue). Adjust speed with the slider or Up/Down.
