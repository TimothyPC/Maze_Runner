Maze Runner
THE MOONSTONE RUINS

Steer to find the glowing portal at the far corner of each of the seven mazes. You have 8 minutes per stage before the way out closes. Restarts switch between five maze styles without repeating the last style.

A hammer hidden in each maze can break one wall. Stage 2 has a drifting ghost beneath a sky full of stars. Stage 3 leads outdoors through gray stone ruins: recover two moon runes to open the gate, avoid stone sentinels and a wandering lich, and use ancient waystones to jump through the maze.

Stage 4 descends into the Glacier Vault: your warmth drains in the cold, so linger by braziers, dodge frost wisps and the ice yeti, and find the Aurora Arch. Stage 5 is the Ember Forge: turn three valves to open the Furnace Gate while iron pistons shift the maze and lava vents erupt underfoot.

Stage 6 opens into the Night Garden: catch four fireflies to light the Rose Gate's lantern, and never turn your back on the topiary gargoyles — they only move when you aren't looking. Stage 7 climbs the Mirror Spire: mirage gates look exactly like the real one, so find the Oracle Lens, and beware the wraiths you can only glimpse.

Hold the left or right side to steer (or use Left/Right or A/D). Tap the center to stop moving, but the timer and enemies keep going. Adjust speed with the slider or Up/Down.
